1. Introduction

KinShift ("we," "our," "us," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and our website.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App or Services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), and password.
  • Profile Information: Optional information such as profile picture, bio, and role preferences.
  • Shift Information: Details about shifts you create or manage, including date, time, location, and participants.
  • Household Information: Information about households you create or join, including household name and member details.
  • Payment Information: Billing information for subscriptions (processed securely through RevenueCat and payment providers).

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique identifiers, mobile network information.
  • App Usage Data: Features used, actions taken, time spent in app, error logs, and crash reports.
  • Location Information: Precise location data only if you grant permission and enable location-based features.
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable data about app usage patterns.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Social Media: If you authenticate via social media, we receive profile information per your consent.
  • Payment Processors: RevenueCat provides subscription and payment information.
Note: We do not collect sensitive personal information such as social security numbers, health records, or financial account details, except for payment processing through secure third-party providers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes:

3.1 Primary Uses

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App and Services
  • To process shift scheduling and household management
  • To manage subscriptions and billing
  • To send transactional communications (verification codes, receipts, etc.)

3.2 Communications

  • Shift notifications and reminders
  • Household updates and member notifications
  • Service announcements and updates
  • Marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Customer support responses

3.3 Analytics and Improvements

  • To understand how users interact with the App
  • To identify and fix bugs and technical issues
  • To improve user experience and features
  • To conduct research and analytics

3.4 Legal and Safety

  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To enforce our Terms of Service
  • To protect against fraud and security threats
  • To protect the rights and safety of our users
Ad Display: We use subscription tier information to determine whether to display advertisements. Users with Premium subscriptions will not see ads. Household admins with Standard subscriptions can disable ads for the entire household by upgrading to Premium.

4. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

4.1 Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocol
  • Firebase Security: Firebase Authentication uses industry-standard security protocols
  • Firestore Database: Data stored with Firebase security rules and encryption at rest
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control (RBAC) limits data access to authorized users

4.2 Administrative Safeguards

  • Restricted employee access to user data
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Secure password requirements
  • Multi-factor authentication for sensitive operations
Limitation of Liability: While we use reasonable efforts to protect your information, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security. You use the App at your own risk.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our Services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. Retention periods vary based on the data type:

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Shift Data: Retained for the duration of the shift and 12 months afterward for record-keeping.
  • Household Data: Retained while the household exists. Deleted when the household is removed.
  • Payment Information: Retained per payment processor policies (RevenueCat). Payment history retained for 7 years for tax purposes.
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized. Retained per Firebase default policies (26 months).
  • Support Communications: Retained for 2 years for customer support purposes.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see Section 10). Some data may be retained if required by law.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Portability

You have the right to request access to your personal information and receive a copy in a portable format. Contact us at the email address below.

6.2 Correction

You can update or correct your account information directly through the App settings. Contact us for assistance if needed.

6.3 Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data. We will delete your information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. Please note that some data may remain in backup systems.

6.4 Opt-Out of Communications

You can opt out of marketing communications by:

  • Unsubscribing via email links in promotional messages
  • Adjusting notification settings in the App
  • Contacting us directly

Note: You cannot opt out of transactional communications necessary for account and service operation.

6.5 Privacy Rights by Region

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights:

  • GDPR (EU/UK): Right to be forgotten, data portability, withdrawal of consent
  • CCPA (California): Right to know, delete, opt-out of sale
  • Other Regulations: Similar rights under applicable local laws

Contact us to exercise any of these rights with information about your location.

8. Children's Privacy

KinShift is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete such information immediately.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have collected information about a child under 13, please contact us immediately at kinshift.support@gmail.com.

Depending on your jurisdiction, different age restrictions may apply (e.g., 16 in GDPR regions). Users under these ages should not use the App without parental consent.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of material changes by:

  • Posting the updated Privacy Policy on our website and App
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Policy
  • Sending you an email notification (for material changes)
  • Requesting your consent (where legally required)

Your continued use of the App following the posting of revised Privacy Policy means you accept and agree to the changes.
